# Gubby Clicker

A free browser-based clicker game made by **Icy Games**. No download. No account. Just open and play.

---

## Features

- **Click to earn** — click Gubby to generate gold and build your power
- **Potion shop** — 12+ potions across 4 tiers for temporary buffs
- **5 worlds** — travel through Peaceful Grasslands, Paradise, Forest of Sins, Frosty Glaciers, and Medieval Times, each with unique currencies and multipliers
- **Prestige system** — ascend to reset and grow stronger with each run
- **Glory Set** — collect 3 rare items and equip them for up to 15x permanent click power
- **VP Tickets** — Bronze, Silver, and Gold tickets that stack click power and sell bonuses
- **Roulette Ticket** — 50/50 bet for a chance to double your gold
- **Skins** — unlock Star, Demon, and Pancake Gubby skin sets
- **Quests** — complete challenges for gold rewards
- **Storage tabs** — manage items and tickets separately
